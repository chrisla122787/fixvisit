How it works

1

Document the visit

Pull up the saved client and property, add the work order and unit numbers, clock in and out, and give every work item a status as you go — completed, diagnosed, or needs a return visit.

2

Photograph & itemize

Snap before and after photos per work item, log issues you found with a severity and your recommendation, and itemize parts, labor, and your call-out fee. Property-manager mode won't call the documentation complete until every finished item has a photo.

3

Submit it, track the payment

Send a branded PDF report — work performed, photos, issues, recommended next steps, and the charges with the amount due and your payment terms — then mark the visit Submitted, Approved, and Paid as the money moves.

The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Your visit records, client details, charges, and photos are stored only in your own browser on your own device — photos in the browser's local database, never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account and no tracking of your clients or photos. Photos take up real space on the device, so export a backup file from the app regularly; clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

A word about photos: you are working inside someone's home. Get the owner's or tenant's permission before photographing a property and before sharing reports that contain photos. Keep personal belongings out of frame — photograph the work and its condition, not people's things. And because your browser is the only place photos live until you export them, download each visit's evidence pack promptly and keep it in your own storage (Drive, Dropbox, or similar) — we never hold a copy, so what's lost from the device is lost for good.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free visit builder keeps working either way.
